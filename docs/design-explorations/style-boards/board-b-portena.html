Cartelería porteña

Rationale. Buenos Aires as a reading experience: black enamel street plates, kiosk lettering, subte tickets and the thin gold scroll-lines of sign painters, all on cream paper with a celeste that looks a little sun-faded. Warm and urban, but kept to three colours and generous white space so it still reads as a professional portfolio.

Type is local too: Montserrat (Julieta Ulanovsky, drawn from the old signs of the Montserrat neighbourhood), Sansita and Archivo (Omnibus-Type) and Chivo Mono (Omnibus-Type). The ornament is original geometry inspired by the filete tradition (a whip line, one spiral, one leaf), never copied from any painter's work.
