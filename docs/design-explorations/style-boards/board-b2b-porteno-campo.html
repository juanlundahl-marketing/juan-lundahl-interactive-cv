De la vereda al campo

Rationale. The same everyday porteño base as B2a (plates, painted lettering, cream paper) opened up to the countryside an Argentine grows up with: the almacén de ramos generales sign, the kilometre post on the ruta, the alambrado running along a pasture, the estancia gate, mate and the pava, leather, earth and yerba. A single original guarda border carries the rural textile memory without copying any traditional design. Ornament stays at one per section; the layout stays clean for recruiters.

Type: Bitter (Huerta Tipográfica) as a sturdy slab for sign-plank headings, Alegreya Sans (Huerta Tipográfica) for warm, readable body text, Montserrat (Julieta Ulanovsky) for plates, route numbers and labels, and Sansita Swashed (Omnibus-Type) for one painted accent word. Behind the hero: a faint, worn celeste-white-celeste painted wall with the sun cropped in the corner, on one dial.

KM13

Cómo se compara con B

B looked at Buenos Aires from a visitor's seat. B2b keeps the porteño base and adds the countryside as it is lived, not as a postcard.

Out (traveller cues)

  • Transit "boleto" credential and punch holes.
  • Generic subte line diagram.
  • "Buenos Aires" souvenir plate under the portrait.
  • Generic vintage-travel cream and celeste.
  • Filete scroll corners on every card.

In (porteño + campo)

  • Almacén libreta credential with a leather "Abierto / Vuelvo enseguida" tag.
  • KM posts for section numbers and achievements; route plates and an alambrado for markets, ending at the tranquera.
  • One original guarda as the only repeating ornament.
  • Leather, earth, yerba and straw beside ink and a weathered celeste; Huerta Tipográfica slab + sans.
  • Worn celeste-white-celeste wall and a cropped sun behind the hero, on one dial.
