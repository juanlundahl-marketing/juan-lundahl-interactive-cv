Celeste y blanco, sobrio.

Rationale. The national colours, read as light rather than as symbols: a Buenos Aires sky over white paper, with a deep ink-blue doing the heavy lifting for text. No suns, flags or emblems; the identity comes from proportion, a single celeste band and calm editorial type.

All three typefaces are Argentine-made and free on Google Fonts: Alegreya and Alegreya Sans (Huerta Tipográfica) and Chivo Mono (Omnibus-Type, Buenos Aires). Celeste is used for surfaces and rules; anything that has to be read sits in ink or deep celeste, so every text pair passes WCAG AA.
