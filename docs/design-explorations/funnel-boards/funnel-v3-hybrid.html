Concept note

The simple funnel, one stage at a time

  • The same four enamel plates as v1 (celeste, sign gold, bondi green, oxblood) form the funnel. Each plate is a button: open one and the others dim, so the eye stays on a single layer.
  • The open stage shows its purpose, "What I do here" and the tools with level pads and where they were used. Wide screens: a card beside the funnel. Phones: an accordion under the plate. Arrow keys, Home and End move between plates; motion is a short fade, removed under reduced motion.
  • Content is read live from data/content.json (en). Board-only curation: stage 4 reads "Retention & Loyalty" with marketing-first copy; AI copilots, AI integration and Power Automate move to the small Stack line; Microsoft, Apple, Slack and Teams are work environments without levels.
