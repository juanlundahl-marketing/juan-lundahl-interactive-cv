Concept note

A plain, classic funnel

  • Four enamel plates (celeste, sign gold, bondi green, oxblood) stack and narrow like a real funnel: the shape alone says "marketing funnel". Each plate keeps the site's chapa details: cream inner line, two rivets, a chipped corner.
  • Beside each plate (below it on phones): one line on the stage's purpose and the tools, each with the site's vereda level pads. No animation, no counters; only a soft hover or focus highlight.
  • Content is read live from data/content.json (en). Board-only curation: stage 4 is labelled "Retention & Loyalty" with a marketing-first line; AI copilots, AI integration and Power Automate move to the small Stack line; Microsoft, Apple, Slack and Teams appear as work environments without levels.
