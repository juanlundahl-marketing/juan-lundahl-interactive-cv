      Board note

      v2a: the funnel as a scroll story

      A large funnel drawn as four stacked enamel plates stays pinned on the left while the stage cards scroll by on the right. Each card fills its plate from the top as you read it, so the funnel is "built" stage by stage; a colectivo roller sign (line number + rolling destination blind + four pips) always says where you are. Click or tab to any plate to jump to its card. Loyalty closes the loop back to awareness with one dashed arrow: no lead dots, no counters.

      • Mobile (< 960px): the big drawing is replaced by the sticky roller sign with a mini four-plate funnel; each card carries its own narrowing enamel band that fills left to right.
      • Reduced motion: every plate is drawn lit, the blind changes without rolling; the sign still tracks the stage.
      • Content: stages, "what I do here", tools, levels and notes come from data/content.json (en). Board proposals: stage 4 is shown as Retention & Loyalty (content says "Retention & Automation"), the one-line stage taglines are new marketing-first copy, and the AI copilots move from stage 4 to an "across the funnel" strip with the work environments (no levels for environments).
      • Complexity / cost: medium. About 350 lines of JS + CSS on top of the site tokens: one SVG built from data, a scroll handler (rAF-throttled, no scroll hijacking) and CSS variables for the fills. Risk is low: no libraries, the sticky column is plain CSS. Porting to js/sections/skills.js: roughly half a day, plus ES/DE stage names on the plates (long German names need the two-line plate label already supported).
