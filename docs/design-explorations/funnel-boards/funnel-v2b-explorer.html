    Board note

    v2b: la cajonera, a funnel of plates you pull out

    The funnel is a rack of four enamel plates, each narrower than the one above, like the stacked street plates on a Buenos Aires corner. Choosing a plate pulls it out of the stack like a drawer: the plates around it part, it lights up in its enamel colour, and its contents open in a large detail plate with "what I do here" and tool cards (level dots, where I used it). Each plate already shows its tool abbreviations, so the whole funnel reads at a glance before anything is opened. A level filter (All / Expert / Advanced / Intermediate) lights the matching tools on every plate at once, so you can see where the expertise sits along the funnel without any invented numbers.

    • Why this one: it stays a recognisable funnel (four narrowing layers, loyalty feeding back to awareness), it reuses the page's own vocabulary (chapas with rivets and inner line, linea numbers, destination sign, offset ochre shadow), and it is honest: everything shown comes from the content file.
    • Interaction: click/tap a plate, or use the keyboard: Tab to the rack, Up/Down (Home/End) moves between plates, Enter/Space opens. Prev/next buttons inside the detail plate on desktop. Screen readers hear the open stage.
    • Mobile (< 900px): the same rack full width; the detail plate opens in place under the chosen plate and the plates below slide down.
    • Reduced motion: no sliding or fading; the open plate simply changes.
    • Content: from data/content.json (en). Board proposals: stage 4 shown as Retention & Loyalty (content says "Retention & Automation"), new marketing-first one-line taglines, and the AI copilots moved to an "across the funnel" strip next to the work environments (environments without levels).
    • Complexity / cost: medium, slightly above v2a. About 400 lines of JS + CSS, no libraries, no SVG geometry (the plates are CSS clip-paths, so they scale to any width and language). Needs care with focus rings on clipped shapes (handled on the list item) and with moving the detail plate on mobile. Porting to js/sections/skills.js: about a day including ES/DE.
